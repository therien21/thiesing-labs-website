How We Work

Start with the shift
We look for moments when new technology makes an old way of doing something obviously wrong. That gap — between what's possible and what most people are still doing — is where we build.
Validate before building
We don't treat a working prototype as proof of anything. Real evidence comes from people, not code. We spend time in the problem before we commit to a solution.
Stay lean on purpose
AI infrastructure has changed what a small team can build. We use that advantage deliberately — staying small keeps us fast, close to users, and free from the organizational weight that slows most companies down.
One problem at a time
Each venture has a single, specific problem it's trying to solve. When we can't say the problem in one sentence, we're not ready to build.

About

Thiesing Labs is an independent venture studio founded by Therien Thiesing. We build across technology, systems, and product design — finding problems at inflection points where new infrastructure makes entirely new products possible.

We're currently focused on the agentic commerce layer — the infrastructure that lets AI assistants discover, evaluate, and transact with businesses on behalf of users. Our flagship venture, Compatible AI, is our first product in this space.
